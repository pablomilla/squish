Snap your meal Plus · try 10 free

Take a photo of your plate. Squish recognises the food, estimates the portions, and works out the nutrition. Change anything before you save it.

Quick snap Plus

No time, or too polite to hold your phone up? One tap on the Quick snap widget in the phone apps, one tap to snap, and your phone goes back in your pocket. Squish logs the meal for you to check later.

🎙️

Say it or type it Plus · try 10 free

“Two slices of toast with peanut butter and a banana.” Describe it in your own words, out loud or typed, and it is logged.

Scan a barcode

Packaged food is a scan away, looked up in Open Food Facts — nothing about you goes with the number. Free, as is searching for any food by name.

Import a recipe Plus

Paste a link to a recipe you are cooking — or, in the phone apps, share it straight from TikTok or Instagram — and Squish works out a portion's nutrition for you.

See your progress

Friendly, not fussy
