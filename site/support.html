Support

Answers to the things people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I start?

Open app.squish.online in any browser, on your phone or your computer. It asks a few questions to set your targets, and you are away. On a phone, add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Do I need an account?

No. Your diary lives on your device. An account is for two things: moving to a new phone without starting again, and getting your diary back if this one is lost. Make one on the You screen whenever you like.

I have a new phone. How do I get my diary back?

Open Squish on the new phone and tap I already have an account on the welcome screen. Sign in, and your diary comes straight back.

I have forgotten my password

On the sign-in screen, tap I have forgotten my password and we will email you a link. It works for two hours. If it does not arrive in a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Squish used to be at squish.online. Where has my diary gone?

Squish moved to app.squish.online. Open squish.online in the same browser you used before and you will see a button that takes your diary with you. If you had an account, you can simply sign in at the new address instead.

How accurate are the photo estimates?

Close enough to guide your choices — they are estimates from a picture, not a laboratory. Squish shows you what it thinks is on the plate and how much, and you can change anything before you save it. For packaged food, scanning the barcode is the most exact.

What do I get with Squish Plus?

The AI: 60 meal analyses a month by photo or description, the nutritionist for questions about your own diary, 10 recipe imports a month, and six wild finishes for Squish — rainbow, holographic, gold, chrome, candy and sunset — and things for Squish to wear, scenes for Home, and frames and stickers for share cards, including seasonal ones. Plus is £6.99 a month or £49.99 a year, and goes on sale with the iPhone and Android apps. Everything you need to keep a diary stays free.

What is free?

Keeping your diary: logging meals by hand, searching for foods, scanning barcodes, your charts and streaks, reminders and export — for good. To see what the AI can do, a free account comes with 5 AI meal analyses to try. They are a one-off taste rather than a monthly allowance, because every analysis costs real money to run.

How do I invite a friend?

Open the You tab and tap Invite a friend, or share a progress card — your invite link goes with it. When your friend has made an account, confirmed their email and used Squish on three different days, you both get a month of Squish Plus. Already on Plus? You get 20 extra photo analyses and 10 extra questions straight away, and the month is saved on the end of your Plus. You can earn up to twelve a year this way. You see how many friends joined, never who.

When are the iPhone and Android apps coming?

Soon. They are being prepared for the App Store and Google Play. Your account works in both, so anything you log in the browser now comes with you.

How do I export or delete my data?

On the You screen: Export saves your whole diary as a file. Delete account removes your account and the copy on our server. Reset clears everything from your device too. The privacy policy explains exactly what is kept and why.

Is Squish medical advice?

No. Squish gives estimates and friendly encouragement to help you make choices. It is for adults, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to your GP before tracking what you eat.
