Getting started

Four steps, a couple of minutes.

  1. Open Squish

    Go to app.squish.online in any browser. On a phone, add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app. The iPhone and Android apps are on their way.

  2. Answer a few questions

    Your goal, how you eat, any allergies and how fast you want to go. Squish works out your daily targets and shows you how. Change them any time on You → Your plan.

  3. Log your first meal

    Tap + and snap your plate, or describe it in your own words. Searching, barcodes and logging by hand are free for good.

  4. Make a free account

    It is optional, but it keeps a copy of your diary, keeps it in step on your other devices, and comes with 10 AI meal analyses and 5 questions for Squish to try.

Open Squish

Every feature, explained

Where to find it, and what it does.

📸 Logging a meal

  • Snap a meal Plus · try 10 free Photograph your plate. Squish names the food and portions and works out the nutrition — change anything before you save.
  • Describe it Plus · try 10 free Type it or say it: “a bowl of porridge with banana”.
  • Search foods and Scan a barcode Everyday foods and packets, free.
  • Saved Foods you have on repeat, logged in a tap. Tap the heart beside a food when you log it to save it.
  • Recipe from a link Plus Paste the page you cooked from and get one portion's nutrition.
  • Quick snap Plus One tap to the camera, one tap to snap, phone away — checked later. A widget in the phone apps; try it from You → Quick snap.

🏠 Your day

  • Home What is left today, your macros, and ideas for what to eat next. Sugar, salt and more opens the rest.
  • Water, steps and weight Tap + on each to add to today.
  • Food quality A score out of 100 for what a meal is made of: 75+ brilliant, 55–74 balanced, 38–54 so-so, under 38 room to improve. Tap a meal to see why it scored what it did.
  • Diary Every day, every meal. Tap a meal to see what was in it, or to change it.
  • Just the essentials Prefer it simple? You → Appearance → How much detail.

💬 Ask Squish Plus · try 5 free

  • Questions “Am I getting enough protein?” Squish reads your diary before it answers.
  • It remembers An allergy, a food you avoid, what you are training for. See and change what it noted on You → Squish's notes.
  • Past chats Pick up where you left off.

🗓️ Meal plans Plus

  • Plan my week On Ask Squish → Meal plan. Add anything else in the box — “vegetarian, nut allergy”.
  • Keep, swap, clear Keep a meal for next week, swap one you don't fancy, or Edit plan to remove meals or clear a day. Every change has Undo.
  • Shopping list Everything the week needs, by aisle. Tick it off as you go.
  • Recipes and cook mode Ingredients, method and a picture for every step. Change how many it serves for the household.
  • I ate this Logs a planned meal in one tap. Plans you skip are fine — Squish learns from what you made.

📈 Progress

  • Insights Weekly, monthly and all-time charts for calories, protein, fibre, sugar, salt and quality.
  • Streaks and badges Days logged in a row, and badges for the things you try.
  • Weight Log it when you like; your targets learn from how your weight actually moves.

👯 Friends

  • Squads Up to five friends cheering each other on, with a goal for the week. They see your streak and badges — never what you ate. You → Friends.
  • Invite a friend When they have joined and used Squish on three days, you both get a month of Plus.

🎩 Make it yours

  • Squish's look Things to wear, scenes for Home, and finishes like rainbow and gold on Plus.
  • Light or dark You → Appearance.
  • Language and units 24 languages, metric or imperial, kcal or kJ: You → About you.
  • Meal reminders In the phone apps, a gentle nudge when a meal is due: You → Meal reminders.

🔒 Your account and data

  • Every device Sign in on each and your diary keeps in step, up to five at once.
  • Export or delete You → Your data. Your diary is yours.
  • Plus and usage What you have used and what is left: You → Plan and usage.

Questions

Answers to the things people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I start?

Open app.squish.online in any browser, on your phone or your computer. It asks a few questions to set your targets, and you are away. On a phone, add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Do I need an account?

No. Your diary lives on your device. An account is for two things: moving to a new phone without starting again, and getting your diary back if this one is lost. Make one on the You screen whenever you like.

I have a new phone. How do I get my diary back?

Open Squish on the new phone and tap I already have an account on the welcome screen. Sign in, and your diary comes straight back.

I have forgotten my password

On the sign-in screen, tap I have forgotten my password and we will email you a link. It works for two hours. If it does not arrive in a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Squish used to be at squish.online. Where has my diary gone?

Squish moved to app.squish.online. Open squish.online in the same browser you used before and you will see a button that takes your diary with you. If you had an account, you can simply sign in at the new address instead.

How accurate are the photo estimates?

Close enough to guide your choices — they are estimates from a picture, not a laboratory. Squish shows you what it thinks is on the plate and how much, and you can change anything before you save it. For packaged food, scanning the barcode is the most exact.

What do I get with Squish Plus?

The AI, unlimited for your own eating under fair use: meal analyses by photo or description, Ask Squish for questions about your own diary and two weekly meal plans a week, recipe imports — and six wild finishes for Squish — rainbow, holographic, gold, chrome, candy and sunset — and things for Squish to wear, scenes for Home, and frames and stickers for share cards, including seasonal ones. Plus is {yearly} a year, with the first 3 days free, or {weekly} a week, and goes on sale with the iPhone and Android apps. Fair use means daily ceilings far past a real day — 40 analyses, 50 questions and 10 recipe imports — which reset at midnight; the terms say more. Everything you need to keep a diary stays free.

What is free?

Keeping your diary: logging meals by hand, searching for foods, scanning barcodes, your charts and streaks, reminders and export — for good. To see what the AI can do, a free account comes with 10 AI meal analyses and 5 questions for Squish to try. They are a one-off taste rather than a monthly allowance, because every analysis costs real money to run.

How do I invite a friend?

Open the You tab and tap Invite a friend, or share a progress card — your invite link goes with it. When your friend has made an account, confirmed their email and used Squish on three different days, you both get a month of Squish Plus. Already on Plus? The month is saved on the end of your Plus. You can earn up to twelve a year this way. You see how many friends joined, never who.

What is a squad?

Up to five friends who cheer each other on. Start one or join with a code on the You tab. Your squad sees your first name, your streak, whether you have logged today, your days this week, your badges and your Squish — never your food or your weight. You send each other cheers from a set list, and when everyone logs on five days in a week, you hit it together. You can leave a squad, or block someone, at any time: blocked people never see you or your cheers again.

When are the iPhone and Android apps coming?

Soon. They are being prepared for the App Store and Google Play. Your account works in both, so anything you log in the browser now comes with you.

How do I export or delete my data?

On the You screen: Export saves your whole diary as a file. Delete account removes your account and the copy on our server. Reset clears everything from your device too. The privacy policy explains exactly what is kept and why.

Is Squish medical advice?

No. Squish gives estimates and friendly encouragement to help you make choices. It is for adults, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, please talk to your GP before tracking what you eat.

What do the colours on the quality scores mean?

Every meal gets a food quality score out of 100, for what it is made of rather than how big it is. Green is brilliant (75 and up), pale green is balanced (55 to 74), amber is so-so (38 to 54), and pink has room to improve. Tap a meal to see what lifted or lowered it.

How do I change or clear Squish's meal plan?

On Ask Squish → Meal plan: tap a meal for its recipe, then Keep to hold on to it for next week or Swap for another. Edit plan lets you pick meals to remove or clear a whole day, and Clear the plan removes the lot. Each comes with Undo.

Will the meal plan avoid my allergies?

Yes. Tell Squish when you set up, on You → Food and goals, or in the box when you plan your week — “nut allergy”, “no shellfish” — and it is never planned in, hidden ingredients included. Always check a label if an allergy is serious: Squish can be wrong.

What is Quick snap?

For when there is no time: one tap opens the camera, one tap takes the photo, and you can put your phone away. Squish reads it in the background and logs it for you to check later — it shows on Home and in the diary as “to check”. In the phone apps it is a widget; you can also try it from You → Quick snap.

Can I use Squish on more than one device?

Yes. Make a free account, sign in on each one, and your diary keeps itself up to date across them within a few seconds. Up to five devices can be signed in at once.
