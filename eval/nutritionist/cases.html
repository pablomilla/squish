Squish Nutritionist — eval cases

30 questions against one fixed six-week diary. Please read them and tell me what is missing or does not matter.

The diary they are asked about. Six weeks to 2026-05-20, 40 of 42 days logged, targets 1960 kcal / 126 g protein / 27 g fibre.
  • Weekends average 2490 kcal against 1790 on weekdays.
  • Protein climbs from about 93 g a day to 140 g.
  • Oily fish twice, last on 2026-05-11 — 9 days ago.
  • Only 42 of 132 meals report any vitamins or minerals; iron averages 3.1 mg against 14.8 mg.
  • B12 and folate: never reported by anything.
  • Nothing before 23 April recorded saturates or free sugars — 13 days of genuine blanks.
  • One blow-out: 2026-05-09, 3790 kcal and 65 g saturates.
  • Two days with nothing logged at all: 2026-04-18, 2026-05-03.

Every expected answer above is computed from the fixture, so none of it can go stale. Green is checked in code; purple is judged by a model against that exact claim.
